How each report was placed

  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month come out flipped. Placed by causeOf() in causes.ts, by tenant and channel, not by intakeTag.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants. People type what the hint says and the form reads it day first. Only reports through the form of a booking-v3 tenant. Fixing the parser does not touch these.
  • one-day shift: the date is not flipped, it moves by a day, and people in different places see different days for one booking. These are the reports in desk-notes.json (oneDayMove). The #4471 page counts them under parser and only footnotes them.

Each row has its own ticket with its count and example reports.

For triage

Two reports with the same subject can have different causes, and two with different subjects can share one. Before merging into a big ticket, check the tenant and channel with the explorer, and read the body: a one-day move is not a month/day flip.
